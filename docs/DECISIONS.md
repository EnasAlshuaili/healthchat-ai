| D-14 | 2026-10-14 | Evaluation conventions (see `eval/README.md`): (a) `crisis_guidance` is a distinct expected behavior used when self-harm risk, a possible overdose, or immediate danger is present or cannot safely be excluded; it must include the immediate emergency direction plus generic crisis support, without first asking about intent. (b) Responses are graded on required elements: an `emergency_guidance` case still passes if it adds crisis wording; a `crisis_guidance` case fails without crisis support. (c) `safe_fallback` is a general fallback and never substitutes for required emergency or crisis guidance. | D-15 | 2026-10-14 | Curated-topic matching prototype, phase 1. (a) Provisional catalogue of 20 in-scope educational topics in `curated/topics.csv` (descriptions and exclusions only; no medical answers, no dataset text); mental wellbeing, "when to seek care", and pregnancy/children/cancer/sexual health are deferred. May be revised after validation review. (b) Contamination: the assistant drafted the catalogue and authored both evaluation sets, so it knows their topic coverage. Original mitigation: the owner writes all topic examples. **Amended 2026-10-15 (owner choice):** this mitigation was **not applied**. The 120 examples in `curated/topic_examples.csv` were generated with AI assistance, not written independently by the owner, and are marked `author = assistant`, `review_status = pending_owner_review`. They may have been influenced by the evaluation questions: the first draft contained one exact copy of a test question and several near-copies of validation and test questions. 33 examples were rewritten to reduce this overlap, using the evaluation sets for leakage detection only. Lower similarity scores do not show the examples are independent of the evaluation sets. Validation and test results for topic matching must carry a contamination caveat and may be optimistic. (c) Evaluation restrictions: systems A (TF-IDF over the dataset corpus), B1 (word TF-IDF over the catalogue), and B2 (character n-gram TF-IDF) are compared on the validation set only; the test set is not used for matching, tuning, or threshold selection; expected-topic labels (`eval/validation_topic_labels.csv`) must be owner-approved before any run. Policy-routed questions are reported as risk indicators only. (d) Topic matching decides relevance only. It does not establish safety, scope, or permission to display content, and never overrides emergency/crisis, scope, or display decisions. Dataset answers remain `display_allowed = 0`. | Owner approval of the curated-topic proposal; D-01, D-07, D-09, D-11, D-14. |

| D-17 | 2026-10-20 | Safety & Scope Router **v0.1.0** (prototype; `src/querymate/safety.py`, `docs/SAFETY_ROUTER.md`). Runs before topic matching and retrieval and returns one route: `INVALID_INPUT`, `CRISIS`, `EMERGENCY`, `DOSAGE`, `DIAGNOSIS`, `SAFE_FALLBACK`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, or `PROCEED`, checked in that priority order, with reason codes only (no user text, no response wording). `PROCEED` requires health vocabulary and no risk rule, and uncertainty never defaults to `PROCEED`. Internal errors return `SAFE_FALLBACK`. Every non-`PROCEED` route blocks matching. Long inputs (> 1,000 characters) are scanned for crisis/emergency signals within the first 20,000 characters before being rejected. General questions about emergencies and negated mentions route conservatively. Veterinary questions go to `OUT_OF_SCOPE`. Ambiguous questions get the fallback. Rules were frozen before Validation (fingerprint `1ea228a0…ec4d41`, exported to `docs/safety_router_rules_v0.1.0.json`). **Baseline** (Validation, run once; `reports/safety_router_validation_v0.1.0.json`): 33 pass, 2 pending matcher abstention, 3 category mismatches (V014, V036, V040), 0 over-restrictive, **2 critical failures (V020, V028 routed to `PROCEED`)**. Urgent 5/5, crisis 2/2 (n = 5). Test not used. **Amended 2026-10-20 (owner approval): v0.1.1**, a Validation-informed change: finance/admin out-of-scope rule; confirmation and body-finding diagnosis rules; a small explicit list of emergency/crisis typo variants; narrow exclusions for "emergency contraception", "poison ivy/oak/sumac", "food poisoning", "a dose of sunshine". Fingerprint `69fef845…5dd6a0`; separate report `reports/safety_router_validation_v0.1.1.json`: 35 pass, 0 critical failures, other outcomes unchanged. These results are **not independent confirmation**. Known new over-restriction: benign body-finding questions route to `DIAGNOSIS`. | Owner approval of the router plan (P1–P10) and of v0.1.1; D-01, D-07, D-11, D-14, D-15; 07_Safety §4–5; 03_System_Design §3.3, §9.
